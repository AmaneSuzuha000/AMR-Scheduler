## Novelty Risk Summary
1. **"PPO selecting among `K = 6` motion primitives (`RAMP-PPO`)"**: Overlaps conceptually with `db-LaCAM` (kinodynamic primitives) + `SJRL`/`CS-PIBT` (learning + safety filter), and **empirically loses to deterministic `R1_Robust` under unified safety**.
2. **"RL choosing conflict-zone passing order (`STCP-RL`)"**: Faces **severe novelty overlap** with **`BTPG-max` (AAAI 2026)** (exact Type-2 passing-order relaxation under delay), **`SJRL` (2026)** / **`Causal PIBT`**, and **`K-ARC` (RA-L 2025)** (event-triggered local coordination). Empirical Task 4 shows `+0.43%` oracle permutation headroom over deterministic rules — clearing neither the headroom gate nor the novelty gate.
